06Process

From idea to product

Seven stages, each ending with something you can review. Nothing disappears into a black box.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business, users and requirements.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Define product scope, architecture and roadmap.

  3. 03

    Design

    Create the user experience and interface system.

  4. 04

    Build

    Develop the product using modern technologies.

  5. 05

    Test

    Test functionality, responsiveness, performance and security.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Deploy the product to production.

  7. 07

    Scale

    Continue improving and expanding the product.
